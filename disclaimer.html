What the site's estimates can and cannot tell you.

Last updated 18 November 2026

This page explains the limits of the information on Elite Football Predictor. Please read it before relying on an article, a calculation, or a model output.

Estimates, not advice

Every probability, expected-goals figure, scoreline estimate, and analytical statement on this site is an estimate. None of it is a recommendation to place a bet, invest money, or make a financial decision. The site does not know your circumstances, your risk tolerance, or your financial position.

No guarantee of accuracy or outcome

We work to make the content clear and the arithmetic correct, but we do not guarantee that any statement is accurate, complete, or current. Football matches are influenced by events that no model can reliably predict, including red cards, injuries, refereeing decisions, weather, and individual errors. A well-reasoned estimate can be wrong.

No invented track record

We do not publish a win rate, a return on investment, or a "proof" record for the model. Any numeric example on the site is illustrative and labelled as such. It is not evidence of future performance. If we ever publish a backtest, we will state the sample, the method, the assumptions, and the limitations.

Data and sources

The interactive model uses numbers entered by the user. It does not fetch live data. Articles may use hypothetical clubs and simplified numbers to explain a concept. We do not claim to have access to a proprietary dataset unless we say so explicitly. Where we link to an external source, we do not control it and cannot guarantee that it remains available or accurate.

Gambling risk

Betting involves financial risk. The built-in margin in market prices means that most people who gamble repeatedly over time lose money. A statistical estimate does not change that. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose, never chase losses, and never treat gambling as a source of income.

The site is intended for adults aged 18 or over. We do not accept bets, process payments, or link to bookmakers. If gambling is causing harm, seek support from the organisations listed on our responsible gambling page.

External links

External links are provided for reference and support. They do not imply endorsement of the entire destination site. We are not responsible for third-party content, products, services, or privacy practices.

Professional advice

Nothing on this site is financial, legal, medical, or psychological advice. If you need help with a gambling problem, a financial difficulty, or a mental health concern, contact a qualified professional or a recognised support service. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Contact

If you believe something on the site is inaccurate or misleading, please tell us through the contact page or email editor@elitefootballpredictor.com.
