Frequently asked questions

Is Elite Football Predictor a betting site?

No. We do not accept bets, process payments, link to bookmakers, or sell tips. The site is an independent football analytics education project with a transparent statistical model.

Does the model guarantee a result?

No. The model produces a probability distribution from the inputs you provide. Football is uncertain, and the model does not know lineups, injuries, weather, or tactical surprises.

Where does the data come from?

The interactive tool uses numbers you enter. Articles use clearly labelled illustrative examples. We do not scrape live match data or present invented records as fact.

Is the site free to use?

Yes. The guides and model are free. If advertising is added later, every slot will be labelled and kept separate from editorial content.

Can I use the estimates for betting?

The site is for education and entertainment, not advice. If you choose to gamble, only risk money you can afford to lose, and seek support if it stops being fun. See our responsible gambling page.
