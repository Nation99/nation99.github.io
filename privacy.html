Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to control cookies and personal data.

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what data this site collects, why it is collected, how long it is kept, and what choices you have. It covers cookies, analytics, advertising, contact messages, and your rights under GDPR and California privacy law.

Who we are

Elite Football Predictor is an independent football analytics and education site. For privacy questions, contact usatonlinetools@gmail.com. The site does not have a public phone number.

This policy applies to https://nation99.github.io and its subpages. It does not apply to third-party sites that we link to, including support organisations and data providers.

Data we collect

Information you send us

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive the name, email address, subject, and message you choose to send. The contact form opens a draft in your email app. The site itself does not store the form contents. Email messages are stored in the email provider's system so that we can reply and keep a record of editorial or privacy requests.

Analytics data

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics 4 may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser type, referral source, and interactions with the site. We use this information to understand which guides are useful and to find technical problems. We do not use analytics to identify you by name.

Advertising data

If advertising is enabled and you accept advertising cookies, Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to measure ads and, where permitted, personalize them. Advertising data may include an approximate location, device information, and browser signals. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details from Google.

Server and security logs

Our hosting provider may process standard server logs, including IP address, request time, requested URL, browser user agent, and error information. These logs are used for security, reliability, and abuse prevention. Retention depends on the hosting provider.

Legal bases for processing

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent for analytics cookies, advertising cookies, and any optional measurement.
  • Legitimate interests for site security, basic technical operation, and responding to messages, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation where we must keep a record or respond to a lawful request.
  • Contract or pre-contract steps if you ask us to provide a service that requires it. The site currently has no paid accounts.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 with the measurement ID G-DHC4SNTD6B. IP anonymization is enabled. Google may process analytics data on servers outside your country. We do not combine analytics data with information that identifies you personally.

You can opt out of Google Analytics across websites by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. You can also block analytics cookies in your browser or reject them in our banner.

Google AdSense and DoubleClick

The site may use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, to limit how often you see an ad, and to measure ad performance. Where required, personalized advertising is used only after you consent.

You can learn more in Google's advertising policies and Google's privacy policy. You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings.

Consent Mode v2

The site sets Google Consent Mode v2 defaults to denied for advertising and analytics storage. If you accept, the site sends a consent update that grants the relevant categories. If you reject, optional storage remains denied and advertising data is redacted where supported. Consent Mode helps Google tags respect your choice, but it does not replace the banner or this policy.

Third-party vendors and opt-outs

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons as a result of ad serving. You can opt out of personalized advertising from many providers through these industry tools:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt-out
  • Your Online Choices (Europe)
  • Google Ads Settings

Opting out of personalized advertising does not mean you will see no ads. It means ads may be less relevant to your interests.

How we use data

  • To operate the site and remember your theme and consent choices.
  • To understand which articles are useful and improve navigation and content.
  • To measure advertising and fund hosting and editing, where ads are enabled.
  • To respond to messages, corrections, and privacy requests.
  • To detect abuse, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your name, email address, or contact message for money. We share data with service providers that help run the site, including hosting, email, analytics, and advertising providers. These providers process data under their own terms and, where required, appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, Google relies on legal transfer mechanisms such as standard contractual clauses. You can read more in Google's privacy policy.

Retention

Contact emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and maintain a record of corrections or privacy decisions. Analytics data is retained according to the Google Analytics settings for the property. Advertising data is retained according to Google's policies. Server logs are retained according to the hosting provider's policy. Consent choices remain in your browser until you clear site data or change your choice.

Your rights under GDPR and UK GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with similar law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of data in certain circumstances.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Request portability of data you provided.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already happened.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

To make a request, email usatonlinetools@gmail.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity before acting. We aim to respond within one month, though complex requests may take longer.

California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, the CCPA and CPRA may give you the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to request correction, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell personal information for money.

Some advertising cookies may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. You can opt out through the cookie banner, Google Ads Settings, or the industry opt-out links above. To exercise other rights, email usatonlinetools@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

Children and age limits

The site is intended for adults aged 18 or over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use HTTPS and reputable service providers, and we keep the site's software updated. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not send sensitive personal information, passwords, or financial details through the contact form or email.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site, the law, or our providers change. The "last updated" date at the top of the page shows the latest revision. Material changes will be highlighted on the site where practical.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: usatonlinetools@gmail.com. You can also use the contact page. For general editorial questions, please use the same address and include the relevant article URL.
